## Eligibility and actual reads

Ordinary io `QueryAccount` can reuse an independently verified metadata origin while rebuilding current financial authority. The origin binds the explicit policy, selected instruments, original verification times, actual private reader generation and epoch, hard invalidation revision, and exact native HTTP and Engine instrument objects. A diagnostic, previous Ready flag, empty account, or elapsed time cannot establish it. Hard changes revoke it; accepted financial changes still revoke current funds and execution readiness.

Warm recovery requires exact owned facts already accepted in this runtime and consumed by ordinary Engine Cache events. It reads complete io open orders, actual fills, and exact active owned CLOID statuses. New, missing, malformed, unowned, saturated, duplicate, or conflicting facts fail closed; warm does not project REST-only fills or statuses, manufacture finality, adopt external orders, or immediately fall back around unknown source debt. Open and terminal journal phases must match compatible actual native order status and events. Worst reservations remain until a complete successful source can absorb them.

The retained native fill ledger lacks a WS/REST ingestion-channel tag. Exact earlier accepted facts and Cache events are required, but their original channel remains unknown when it was not recorded. A verified owned Filled marker missing actual trades can retain an unchanged metadata origin only through its narrow typed failure classification; it never grants Ready or manufactures trades. Earlier WS marker-before-fill hard invalidation remains a cold/Unknown case.

One complete actual account group verifies role, repeated account modes, collateral, full io positions and funds. Selected remote metadata must match the original origin. Each present selected position must counterverify its actual isolated leverage. A malformed present row fails rather than becoming an absent-row shortcut. Each absent selected coin requires two bounded actual `activeAssetData` responses with exact user/coin, isolated mode, positive policy-compliant leverage, agreement with each other, and agreement with the original leverage. These are counterproofs: they do not refresh original metadata times, mark, capacity or availability, or grant new money authority.

## Commit, ownership and retention

HTTP responses are staged without financial emissions. A normal runner TimeEvent, registered on the Cache owner thread, returns completion to that thread. Final commit holds ingress, account and execution guards in order, without an HTTP await. It checks generation, epoch, received sequence and applied state; exact private financial facts and revisions; policy, original metadata and both native instrument stores; expected durable facts; and current ordinary Cache orders, fill events and positions. Deadline and TTL checks include lock waits and durable absorption. A failed or late commit preserves conservative reservations and debt.

Single-flight coalescing requires the same captured source identity. Cancellation, stopped/dropped clients, late callbacks, closed or undrained queues and wrong-thread dispatch cannot grant Ready. Cleanup belongs to its own query token. Owner callbacks hold execution state weakly; abandoned callback registrations cannot themselves retain the journal descriptor. Staged observations remain owned by flight RAII until committed or preserved as unknown debt.

Every retained original body carries actual request JSON and native receive time, with captured query token, generation, epoch, sequence, account, address and policy. Retention precedes parsing and response-item quota waits. The first readonly Unknown debt remains immutable even after reader replacement or loss of admission eligibility. Accepted body and group bounds are 1 MiB and 4 MiB; a debt record that cannot fit its finite retention bound records explicit retention failure rather than claiming original bytes were saved. Transport and other legacy paths retain their existing limits; this is not a global memory-cap claim.

A normal successful metadata origin with no Cache binding can acquire that binding once from actual matching current Cache objects. Existing bindings are immutable until a new normal metadata verification. Connect can remain read-only when restart Cache binding fails; durable native projection debt is not cleared. Owner NewRisk pre-prepare may complete an eligible missing binding, then must satisfy independent metadata, funds, ownership and budget gates. Binding alone grants no Ready.

## Quota and finite economics

Existing limiter capacity/refill, account and metadata TTLs, recovery deadline, action/cleanup budgets and journal caps are unchanged. Application #43 uses 1200 tokens/minute, 20 tokens/second, account 2000 ms, metadata 30000 ms and recovery 3000 ms. Quota waits, retries, item costs and owner completion consume actual bounds; insufficient capacity fails closed.

The source-derived execution minimum is `222 + 2N + 40A`, where N is queried active owned CLOIDs and A is selected symbols absent from the final account's actual leverage rows. Examples are 224 for one owned CLOID with actual position leverage, 262 for one flat selected symbol, and 264 for a flat symbol with one active CLOID. Economic first empty history pages add at least 80 separately. These are lower bounds, not measured bucket balances, continuous liveness guarantees or paired acceptance results.

Successful warm recovery preserves earlier actual finite history ends. A later unrequested interval is explicitly pages-zero, incomplete Unknown. Hard financial/unsupported sources, reader gaps and cold recovery retain independent history demand and conservative failure behavior. A total history deadline can expire during real prerequisite metadata/quota work before a history endpoint reaches the peer; this remains a new source-bound incomplete window, not successful coverage or skipped demand. Raw receipts and fees are not replayed or upgraded. Current account authority and finite funding/ledger coverage remain separate claims.
