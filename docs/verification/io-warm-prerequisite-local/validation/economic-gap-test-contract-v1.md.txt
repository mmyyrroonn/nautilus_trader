## Direct durable contract cases

Six new rstest functions expand to nine declared cases:

- `warm_demand_three_boundaries_append_durable_unknown_tails_and_reopen`: actual synthetic domain observations produce funding and fee receipts, then three increasing demand boundaries preserve the complete prefix and append exactly two zero-page/zero-record incomplete tails. Each group has one revision increment. Stop/drop and reopen retain exact rows/raw/receipts/recognized amounts.
- `warm_demand_same_or_earlier_boundary_is_a_byte_exact_revision_noop`: equal, earlier and zero times preserve the whole visible snapshot and actual checkpoint bytes, including revision and receipts.
- `warm_demand_clips_to_policy_end_and_never_rewrites_the_final_boundary`: later demand clips to the fixed policy end, preserves the first tail, and further elapsed time is an exact no-op.
- `warm_demand_scope_and_endpoint_do_not_borrow_other_or_stream_gap_boundaries` (three cases): funding and ledger have independent starts; different generation and epoch cannot borrow prior ends. A stream-gap diagnostic, a nonzero-page incomplete row or a nonzero-record incomplete row cannot become a represented warm-demand boundary.
- `warm_demand_full_capacity_rejects_both_endpoints_atomically_but_accepts_noop` (two cases): zero or one spare row keeps an already represented boundary a byte/revision no-op, but rejects a later two-endpoint addition without partial state/bytes, taint, raw or receipt changes. Reopen confirms the durable prefix. Original row/byte caps are not enlarged.
- `warm_demand_noop_cannot_bypass_a_real_tainted_checkpoint_failure`: a normal observation that exceeds the original finite observation bound produces an actual tainted persistence refusal. Equal/earlier demand still errors without clearing taint or changing prior checkpoint bytes. It does not manually set a taint flag or change production/store rules.

The direct tests use the existing synthetic economic domain entry points and real temporary durable files. They do not inject financial Engine/Cache events. Existing storage corruption/byte/I/O tests remain; these additions do not claim new storage-mechanism coverage.
