## Finding

Both actual Hyperliquid warm account recoveries committed successfully, including the reduce-only close. The subsequent economic demand-boundary record failed. The observed native diagnostic is `Economic unrequested coverage could not be preserved`. Source analysis identifies an attempt to replace an already durable Unknown coverage row, conflicting with the store's append-only prefix invariant. The precise inner error, `Economic source/consumer state regression`, is a source-derived diagnosis; it was not independently printed in the original child log.

This differs from the historical native115 paired failure, where the close warm transaction itself exhausted its total deadline. The native117 close transaction completed before the original prerequisite expiry. Quota exhaustion, source expiry, unapplied ingress, and raw recovery debt are not supported as the first native117 failure cause.
