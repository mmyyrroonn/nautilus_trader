# Packaging helper correction

The first `write_acceptance.py` invocation failed before creating any output:
Windows CPython defaulted to GBK when reading the UTF-8 Cargo log and raised
`UnicodeDecodeError: 'gbk' codec can't decode byte 0x93 in position 38`.
The helper now supplies UTF-8 explicitly. Product source, frozen source identity,
tests and artifact provenance were unaffected. This was a packaging-helper
failure, not a product regression or an executed acceptance test. Its exact
invocation interval was not separately captured and is not reconstructed.
